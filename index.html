Introduction

I am a solo indie game developer, a programmer, a music composer, and a novice artist studying Computer Science. I bridge the gap between technical engineering and creative audio & visual design. My goal is to make games feel cohesive where the code runs smoothly, the mechanics feel snappy, and the music pulls players straight into the atmosphere along with visuals. And to prove that working solo is not impossible, just takes time, motivation, and effort.
